# 02 — Workforce IAM Risk Assessment

This stage demonstrates how a GRC analyst documents and evaluates **potential** workforce identity and access risks.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [Risk-Assessment-Methodology.docx](Risk-Assessment-Methodology.docx) | Definitions for likelihood, impact, 5×5 scoring, inherent/residual risk and uncertainty |
| [Cyber-Risk-Register.xlsx](Cyber-Risk-Register.xlsx) | Illustrative risk scenarios, scoring and risk-tracking fields; includes **Risk Register**, **Read Me** and **Risk Matrix** worksheets |

**Important:** The register is a training artifact. Risk scenarios and ratings do **not** represent actual findings or verified risk ratings for the University of Melbourne. Do not treat assumed control effectiveness as tested evidence.

**Suggested review:** read the methodology first, then the workbook's Read Me and Risk Matrix tabs, then examine the scenarios in Risk Register.
